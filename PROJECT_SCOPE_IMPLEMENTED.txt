UFS STAYCONNECT - CSIQ 3784 SCOPE IMPLEMENTATION
=================================================

Roles
-----
ADMIN
- Manage customer accounts: create, view, edit, activate/deactivate, reset password and safe deletion.
- Manage Financial Officers: create/authorize, activate/deactivate and safe deletion.
- View/manage accommodation, rooms, services and residence-specific charges.
- Review accommodation applications and allocate an available room when approving.
- Reject applications with a reason.
- Payment management: approve/reject customer payments.
- Payment Types management.
- Reports with filters and CSV export.
- Notifications with unread count.
- Officer Advice & Inputs: review financial officer recommendations and respond.

FINANCIAL OFFICER
- Login and own password change through Account/Profile.
- Customer management: create, view, edit, activate/deactivate and reset customer password.
- View accommodation applications and supporting PDFs.
- Payment verification: approve/reject payments.
- Transactions/payment history.
- Reports and CSV export.
- Customer ratings and reviews.
- Notifications with unread count.
- Advice & Inputs: submit operational/financial advice to the Administrator.

CUSTOMER
- Register/login/profile/password change.
- Browse/filter accommodation and view facilities, rooms and configured charges.
- Submit complete application with ID, registration and funding PDFs.
- Apply to multiple residences while applications are pending.
- Cannot submit a new application after one has been approved.
- Cannot submit duplicate active applications for the same residence.
- Customer can withdraw a pending application.
- Administrator approval allocates a room; other pending applications are automatically closed.
- View application status and documents.
- View configured charges, due dates and payment status.
- Pay one, several or all current charges by Card/EFT; card details are not stored.
- Payment remains Pending until staff verification.
- View payment history and paid receipts.
- Receive application/payment/reminder notifications.
- Submit/update a residence review after approval.

CREATIVE / REALISTIC FEATURES
-----------------------------
- One-approved-accommodation rule.
- Automatic closure of alternative pending applications after room allocation.
- Room occupancy automatically updates accommodation availability.
- Residence-specific payment charges and activation/deactivation.
- Recurring charge due dates and payment-cycle matching.
- Before-due and overdue payment reminders with duplicate-reminder protection.
- Customer dashboard shows overdue and upcoming payment attention.
- Financial reports can be filtered and exported to CSV.
- Financial officer advice/input workflow with administrator response.
- Historical accounts with financial/application history are protected from destructive deletion.
- Application declaration confirms document ownership/accuracy before submission.
