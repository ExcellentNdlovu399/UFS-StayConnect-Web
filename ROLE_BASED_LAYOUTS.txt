UFS StayConnect - Role-Based Layout Structure

Public / anonymous:
    Views/Shared/_Layout.cshtml
    Used by Home, Login, Register and anonymous public pages.

Customer:
    Views/Shared/_CustomerLayout.cshtml

Admin:
    Views/Admin/_AdminLayout.cshtml

Financial Officer:
    Views/FinancialOfficer/_FinancialOfficerLayout.cshtml

Global selection:
    Views/_ViewStart.cshtml

Important:
- Home/Index.cshtml explicitly keeps the public landing layout.
- HomeController already redirects authenticated users to their role dashboard.
- Authenticated views without a hard-coded Layout now inherit the correct role layout.
- The public landing navbar/footer are not placed inside customer/admin/financial-officer layouts.
